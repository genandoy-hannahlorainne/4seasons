Terms of Use

By accessing and using the PDMHS Medical Record System, you agree to the following terms:

1. Authorized Use

This system is exclusively for authorized personnel of PDMHS including students, advisers, clinic staff, and administrators.

2. Confidentiality

All health information is strictly confidential. Users must not share student health data with unauthorized parties.

3. Account Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their login credentials.

4. Acceptable Use

The system must only be used for legitimate school health management purposes.

5. Compliance

All users must comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Privacy Policy

PDMHS Medical Record System is committed to protecting privacy in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Information We Collect

We collect student health information, medical visit records, immunization data, and other health-related information.

How We Use Your Information

Data is used solely for health monitoring and student well-being within the school premises.

Data Security

We implement appropriate measures to protect personal health information against unauthorized access.

Your Rights

Students and guardians have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of personal health data.
