Terms of Use

By accessing and using the PDMHS Medical Record System, you agree to the following terms:

1. Authorized Use

This system is exclusively for authorized personnel of President Diosdado Macapagal High School including students, advisers, clinic staff, and administrators.

2. Confidentiality

All health information accessed through this system is strictly confidential. Users must not share, disclose, or distribute any student health data to unauthorized parties.

3. Account Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their login credentials. Any unauthorized access must be reported immediately to the system administrator.

4. Acceptable Use

The system must only be used for legitimate school health management purposes. Misuse, tampering, or unauthorized modification of records is strictly prohibited.

5. Compliance

All users must comply with applicable Philippine laws including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Privacy Policy

PDMHS Medical Record System is committed to protecting the privacy of all users in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Information We Collect

We collect student health information, medical visit records, immunization data, and other health-related information necessary for school health management.

How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used solely for health monitoring, medical record management, and ensuring student well-being within the school premises.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal health information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Retention

Student health records are retained for the duration of enrollment and as required by DepEd regulations.

Your Rights

Students and guardians have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of personal health data in accordance with applicable laws.
