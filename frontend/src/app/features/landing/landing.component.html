About PDMHS

President Diosdado Macapagal High School (PDMHS) is committed to providing quality education and comprehensive healthcare services to our students. Our Medical Record System streamlines health information management, ensuring efficient and secure access to student medical records.

This system enables our clinic staff, advisers, and administrators to maintain accurate health records, track medical visits, manage emergency drills, and ensure the well-being of every student in our care.
