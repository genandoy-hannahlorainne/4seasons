About Us

President Diosdado Macapagal High School

President Diosdado Macapagal High School (PDMHS) is a public secondary school located in Taguig City, Philippines. Established in 2007, PDMHS is committed to providing quality education and fostering holistic development for every student.

Our Motto
Empowering Young Minds, Touching Lives

Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

As a learner-centered public institution, the Department of Education continuously improves itself to better serve its stakeholders.

Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where:

  • Students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment.
  • Teachers facilitate learning and constantly nurture every learner.
  • Administrators and staff, as stewards of the institution, ensure an enabling and supportive environment for effective learning to happen.
  • Family, community, and other stakeholders are actively engaged and share responsibility for developing life-long learners.

Terms of Use

These Terms of Use govern access to and use of the PDMHS Medical Record System, operated by President Diosdado Macapagal High School. By accessing, using, or interacting with the System, you expressly acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to comply with these Terms. If you do not agree, you must immediately discontinue use of the System.

1. Authorized Users

Access to the System is strictly restricted to duly authorized members of the School community, including enrolled students, class advisers, clinic personnel, and designated system administrators.

Any unauthorized access, attempted access, or misuse of the System by individuals without proper authorization is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action, civil liability, and/or criminal prosecution under applicable laws.

2. Nature of Data and Information

The System collects, processes, and stores personal data and sensitive personal information, including but not limited to:

  • Personal identification details
  • Medical history and clinical records
  • Immunization records
  • Health declarations and screening forms
  • Medical consultation and visit logs
  • Emergency contact information

All such data are classified as confidential and must be handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws and ethical standards.

3. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure

All information accessed through the System is strictly confidential. Users shall not disclose, reproduce, distribute, or transmit any personal or health-related information to unauthorized individuals or entities without proper authority or lawful basis. This obligation of confidentiality shall continue indefinitely, even after termination, suspension, or revocation of access rights.

4. Role-Based Access Control

Access to System features and data is governed by role-based permissions:

  • Students – May access, review, and submit their own health-related information and declarations.
  • Advisers – May access summarized health status of students within their assigned sections solely for monitoring and coordination.
  • Clinic Staff – Authorized to encode, update, and manage medical records and clinical data in the course of official duties.
  • System Administrators – Responsible for system maintenance, configuration, user access management, and enforcement of data governance policies.

Users are strictly prohibited from accessing, modifying, or attempting to access data beyond their authorized permissions.

5. Account Security

Users are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials. Credentials must not be shared, transferred, or disclosed to any other individual. Users must immediately report any suspected unauthorized access, data breach, or security incident. The School shall not be liable for any damages resulting from negligence or failure to secure user credentials.

6. Acceptable Use Policy

The System shall be used exclusively for legitimate school health management purposes. Prohibited activities include, but are not limited to:

  • Unauthorized data access or extraction
  • Use of data for personal, commercial, or non-school purposes
  • Harassment, discrimination, or misuse of sensitive information
  • Any act that violates school policies, DepEd regulations, or applicable laws

7. Data Accuracy and Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that all submitted or encoded information is accurate, complete, and up-to-date. Submission of false, misleading, or incomplete information constitutes a violation of these Terms and may result in disciplinary sanctions.

8. System Monitoring and Audit

All System activities are subject to logging, monitoring, and auditing for security, compliance, and operational purposes. By using the System, users expressly consent to such monitoring. Audit logs may be used as evidence in administrative, disciplinary, or legal proceedings.

9. Compliance with Laws and Regulations

All users must comply with:

  • Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations
  • Applicable Philippine laws and government regulations
  • Department of Education (DepEd) policies
  • Internal School policies on data privacy, ethics, and information security

10. Violations and Sanctions

Violation of these Terms may result in:

  • Immediate suspension or revocation of System access
  • Administrative or disciplinary action under School and DepEd policies
  • Civil and/or criminal liability under applicable laws

The severity of sanctions shall depend on the nature and extent of the violation.

11. Amendments

The School reserves the right to modify or update these Terms at any time. Any changes shall take effect immediately upon posting or notification. Continued use of the System constitutes acceptance of the revised Terms.

12. Governing Law

These Terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

13. Data Protection Commitment

The School is committed to safeguarding personal and sensitive information by implementing appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of data.

14. Contact and Reporting

For concerns regarding data privacy, system access, or security incidents, users must immediately contact the designated School System Administrator.

Privacy Policy

President Diosdado Macapagal High School is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and integrity of personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

This Privacy Policy explains how the PDMHS Medical Record System collects, uses, stores, and protects personal and sensitive personal information.

1. Information We Collect

The System collects and processes the following types of information:

Personal Information

  • Full name, student ID, grade level, section
  • Contact details and emergency contact information

Sensitive Personal Information

  • Medical history and health conditions
  • Immunization and vaccination records
  • Medical consultation and clinic visit logs
  • Health declaration and screening forms

Only data necessary for legitimate school health management purposes are collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected data are used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Monitoring and managing student health conditions
  • Recording and documenting medical consultations and treatments
  • Supporting school clinic operations and emergency response
  • Ensuring compliance with health and safety regulations
  • Facilitating communication with students, parents, or guardians when necessary

Data will not be used for unrelated purposes without prior consent, unless required by law.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

The processing of personal data is based on:

  • Compliance with legal obligations under Philippine law
  • Protection of vital interests (health and safety of students)
  • Legitimate educational and institutional interests of the School
  • Consent of the student or their parent/guardian, where applicable

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Personal and health information shall not be disclosed to unauthorized parties. Data may only be shared under the following circumstances:

  • With authorized school personnel (e.g., advisers, clinic staff) on a need-to-know basis
  • With parents or legal guardians, where applicable
  • When required by law, regulation, or lawful order of authorities
  • In emergency situations where disclosure is necessary to protect life or health

5. Data Retention

Personal data shall be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill its intended purpose or comply with legal requirements. After the retention period, data will be securely deleted, anonymized, or disposed of in a manner that prevents unauthorized access or recovery.

6. Data Security

The School implements appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures, including:

  • Role-based access control
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Data encryption and secure storage
  • System monitoring and audit logs

These measures are designed to protect data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Students and their parents/guardians have the following rights:

  • Right to Access – Request access to personal data stored in the System
  • Right to Rectification – Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking – Request deletion or suspension of data processing, subject to legal limitations
  • Right to Object – Object to certain types of data processing
  • Right to Data Portability – Obtain a copy of data in a usable format, where applicable
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint – File a complaint with the appropriate authority in case of privacy violations

Requests must be submitted to the School's designated Data Protection Officer.

8. Data Security Incident Management

In the event of a data breach or security incident, the School shall:

  • Take immediate steps to contain and assess the breach
  • Notify affected individuals and relevant authorities when required
  • Implement corrective actions to prevent recurrence

9. Policy Updates

The School reserves the right to update or revise this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in legal, regulatory, or operational requirements. Users will be notified of significant changes where appropriate.

10. Contact Information

For any privacy concerns, data requests, or complaints, please contact the designated School System Administrator).
