## 4. SCORING GUIDE

### Likert Scale Interpretation

| Mean Score Range | Verbal Interpretation |
|------------------|----------------------|
| 4.21 - 5.00 | Strongly Agree / Very Important |
| 3.41 - 4.20 | Agree / Important |
| 2.61 - 3.40 | Neutral / Moderately Important |
| 1.81 - 2.60 | Disagree / Slightly Important |
| 1.00 - 1.80 | Strongly Disagree / Not Important |

### Reliability Testing

The survey instruments will undergo reliability testing using **Cronbach's Alpha** with the following interpretation:

| Cronbach's Alpha | Internal Consistency |
|------------------|---------------------|
| α ≥ 0.90 | Excellent |
| 0.80 ≤ α < 0.90 | Good |
| 0.70 ≤ α < 0.80 | Acceptable |
| 0.60 ≤ α < 0.70 | Questionable |
| 0.50 ≤ α < 0.60 | Poor |
| α < 0.50 | Unacceptable |

**Minimum acceptable Cronbach's Alpha:** 0.70

### Sample Size for Pilot Testing

| Respondent Type | Grade 7 | Grade 11 | Total |
|-----------------|---------|----------|-------|
| Students | 15 | 15 | 30 |

### Data Collection Method

- Pre-Implementation surveys administered BEFORE system demonstration
- Purpose: Establish baseline perceptions of current clinic system
- Results used to validate survey instrument reliability

## APPENDIX: SURVEY ADMINISTRATION GUIDELINES

### For Survey Administrators

1. **Before Distribution:**
   - Ensure parent consent forms have been collected for student participants
   - Prepare sufficient copies of the survey questionnaire
   - Arrange a quiet location for survey completion

2. **During Administration:**
   - Explain the purpose of the survey
   - Assure respondents of confidentiality
   - Allow 15-20 minutes for completion
   - Be available to clarify questions without influencing responses

3. **After Collection:**
   - Check for completeness
   - Code responses numerically for analysis
   - Enter data into spreadsheet for Cronbach's Alpha computation

### Ethical Considerations

- Participation is voluntary
- Respondents may skip questions they are uncomfortable answering
- All data will be kept confidential
- Results will be reported in aggregate form only
- Raw data will be securely stored and disposed of after the study
