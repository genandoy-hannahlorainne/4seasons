# Health Monitor - Quick Guide for Advisers

## 🎯 What is Health Monitor?

A visual dashboard that shows clinic visit patterns in your advisory class, helping you spot potential health issues before they spread.

## 🚀 How to Access

1. Login to your adviser account
2. Click **"Health Monitor"** in the top navigation bar
3. View your class health data instantly

## 📊 Understanding the Heat Map

### Color Meanings
| Color | Percentage | What It Means | Action Needed |
|-------|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| 🔲 Gray | 0% | No visits | None - all good! |
| 🔵 Blue | 1-5% | Low activity | Normal - keep monitoring |
| 🟡 Yellow | 6-10% | Moderate | Watch for patterns |
| 🟠 Orange | 11-15% | High activity | Attention needed |
| 🔴 Red | >15% | Critical | Take immediate action |

### Reading the Heat Map
Each cell shows:
- **Date**: Day of the week
- **Percentage**: % of class that visited clinic
- **Student Count**: Number of students

**Example**:
```
Feb 2
15%
6 students
```
This means 6 students (15% of your class) visited the clinic on February 2nd.

## ⚠️ Understanding Alerts

### High Priority (Red)
**What**: >15% of class visited with similar symptoms
**Example**: "15% of class visited clinic on Friday with Respiratory symptoms"
**Action**: 
- Coordinate with janitorial staff for deep cleaning
- Monitor remaining students for symptoms
- Consider informing parents

### Medium Priority (Yellow)
**What**: Trending symptom affecting >10% of students
**Example**: "Respiratory affecting 12% of students"
**Action**:
- Monitor affected students
- Coordinate with health staff
- Watch for spread

## 🤝 When to Take Action

### Immediate Action Needed
- Red alert appears
- >15% of class affected
- Same symptom in multiple students
- Rapid increase in visits

**Do**: Contact health staff, coordinate cleaning, monitor closely

### Monitor Closely
- Yellow/orange days
- 6-15% of class affected
- Trending symptoms
- Multiple days of activity

**Do**: Watch for patterns, prepare for action, inform health staff

### Normal Monitoring
- Blue/gray days
- <5% of class affected
- Isolated incidents
- No patterns

**Do**: Continue regular monitoring

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Why is my heat map empty?**
A: No students visited the clinic in the selected time period. This is good news!

**Q: What if I see a red day?**
A: Click it for details, then follow the recommended actions in the alert.

**Q: Can I see data from other classes?**
A: No, you only see your advisory class for privacy and relevance.

**Q: How often is data updated?**
A: Real-time. Refresh the page to see the latest data.

**Q: What counts as a "visit"?**
A: Any recorded clinic visit by your students, regardless of reason.
