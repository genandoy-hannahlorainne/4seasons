## INSTRUMENT 5: INTERVIEW GUIDE FOR CLINIC STAFF

### Title: "In-Depth Interview: Current Clinic Operations and System Requirements"

**Duration:** 20-30 minutes  
**Method:** Face-to-face or virtual
**Interviewer:** Research Team

---

### OPENING STATEMENT
"Thank you for taking time to speak with us. We are researching ways to improve the school clinic through a digital system called StudentCare+. Your experience is valuable and will help us understand what you need. Everything you share is confidential."

---

### SECTION 1: CURRENT WORKFLOW (10 minutes)

**Q1.1** Can you walk me through your typical day in the clinic? What are the main activities?

*[Listen for: Record-keeping, visits, notification, communication challenges]*

**Q1.2** How do you currently manage student medical records?

*[Probe: Physical files, organization, storage, access, searching]*

**Q1.3** What is the most time-consuming part of your job?

*[Listen for: Paperwork, finding records, communication, documentation]*

**Q1.4** How do you handle medical emergencies? Walk me through that process.

*[Probe: Record access, parent notification, adviser notification, speed]*

---

### SECTION 2: CURRENT PROBLEMS (10 minutes)

**Q2.1** What are the biggest challenges you face in managing medical records?

*[Probe: Lost files, slow retrieval, incomplete information, inconsistency]*

**Q2.2** Are there times when you couldn't find important medical information quickly?

*[Listen for: Emergency situations, allergy information, medication history]*

**Q2.3** How do you currently communicate with parents about medical issues?

*[Probe: Methods (phone, written, etc), speed, reliability, accuracy]*

**Q2.4** Do you face any challenges notifying advisers/teachers about student health?

*[Listen for: Communication gaps, delayed alerts, forgotten notifications]*

**Q2.5** What would make your job easier?

*[Open-ended, listen for: Digital system, faster access, easier communication]*

---

### SECTION 3: SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS (10 minutes)

**Q3.1** If we could design the perfect clinic system, what features would it have?

*[Listen for: QR, SMS, digital records, search, notifications]*

**Q3.2** How important is quick access to student medical records? Why?

*[Probe: Emergency situations, time-saving, efficiency]*

**Q3.3** Would you use QR code scanning to retrieve student records?

*[Listen for: Acceptance, concerns, preferences]*

**Q3.4** How would you feel about sending parent notifications via SMS?

*[Probe: Feasibility, preferences, reliability]*

**Q3.5** What would make you feel comfortable using a digital system?

*[Listen for: ease of use, training, support, trust]*

---

### SECTION 4: CONCERNS & FINAL THOUGHTS (5 minutes)

**Q4.1** What are your main concerns about switching to a digital system?

*[Listen for: Fears, resistance, technical barriers, security concerns]*

**Q4.2** Do you have any final thoughts or suggestions?

*[Open-ended]*

---

### CLOSING
"Thank you very much for your time and insights. Your feedback will help us create a system that truly supports your work. If we need any clarification, may we follow up with you?"

## HOW TO ADMINISTER

### Clinic Staff (2 instruments)
1. **Survey 1** - First, get baseline data about problems
2. **Interview** - After, get detailed feedback
3. **After system demo:** Usability Form
4. **After implementation:** Satisfaction Scale

### Parents (1 instrument)
1. **Survey via:** Online link, WhatsApp, paper form
2. **Timing:** Before and after SMS feature
3. **Optional:** Brief phone interviews for deep insights

### Advisers (2 instruments)
1. **Survey** - Understand current challenges
2. **Usability Form** - After prototype demo
3. **Satisfaction Scale** - After testing

### Students (1 instrument)
1. **Survey** - In class or online
2. **Optional:** Focus group discussion (5-8 students)

## DATA ANALYSIS PLAN

### Quantitative (Likert Scale Responses)
- **Mean & Standard Deviation** per item
- **Frequency Distribution** (% answering each option)
- **Comparison** between groups (staff vs. parents vs. advisers)
- **Acceptance Rate** (% answering 4+5)

### Qualitative (Open-ended responses)
- **Thematic Coding:** Group similar responses
- **Frequency:** How many mentioned each theme
- **Direct Quotes:** Use in final report
- **Recommendations:** Convert feedback to action items
